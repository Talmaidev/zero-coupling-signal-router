## Key Architecture
- 27 contract headers in `include/zcsr/` (only thing modules may cross-include)
- 17 modules in `modules/`
- Zero heap: no malloc/free in zcsr source (system libs excluded)
- Zero coupling: `tools/check_no_cross_includes.sh` enforces
- Platform backends: Win32/WGL/WASAPI, Wayland/EGL/PipeWire, Android/EGL/AAudio, Cocoa/NSOpenGL/AudioUnit
- Thread model: main thread + dedicated workers (audio/decode/PipeWire control), NO thread pool
- Surface pool: 4 slots, main-thread-only, not thread-safe
